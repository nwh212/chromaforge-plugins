- **Open** the Remove tool (`H`) from the tool strip.
- **Modes**: **Remove** (the app picks the source), **Heal**, **Clone**, **Content-Aware** (partial: rebuilt from patches of the surroundings), and **Generative**. `⌥⇧Q` cycles the selected spot's mode.
- **Click or paint** on the blemish. The target circle and its **source** circle appear; drag either to move it. **Refresh source** picks another.
- **Size**, **Feather**, **Opacity** sliders; `[` and `]` change the size.
- **Select** a spot by clicking its pin; `Delete` removes it. **Delete all spots** is at the bottom.
- **Visualize Spots** (`A`) shows a threshold view that makes dust and blemishes stand out; **Tool Overlay** (Auto / Always / Never).
- **Remove Dust** finds soft dust shadows and adds spots for them. It can also run after import. See [Sensor and plug-ins](../sensor-and-plugins/).
- **Remove Film Dust**, for a scan from a film scanner with an infrared pass (VueScan "64-bit RGBI", SilverFast HDRi): fills in the dust, hairs and scratches that pass shows, before the picture is converted, as the scanner's own infrared cleaning does. It is a develop setting: **Film Dust Sensitivity** sets how far below its local level the pass must fall to count, **Film Dust Expand** grows or shrinks each mark before it is filled, and **Show infrared mask** tints what will be filled. **Show as Mask** makes the same marks a mask of the photo, shown as the mask overlay: brush more in or out, then **Fill Selected Mask** or **Generate into Selected Mask** removes what it covers (a large fibre, say). A scan without the pass (a camera scan) has **Film Dust Spots** instead: the picture's smooth areas are searched for the sharp bright marks and each gets a spot filled from its surroundings.
- **Find Blemishes**: spots on skin in faces (beyond Lightroom). See [Retouch](../../ai/retouch/).
- Spots are part of the photo's settings: they copy with **Copy Edit Settings** and sync to other photos.
- Generative remove: [Generative remove](../../ai/generative-remove/).

## Red eye

<Shot name="redeye" alt="The Red Eye panel with Red Eye and Pet Eye buttons" menu="Window ▸ Edit Panels ▸ Red Eye" />

- **Red Eye** tool: drag over an eye; the pupil is found automatically.
- **Pet Eye** adds a catchlight control. Delete a correction by selecting it.

## Dodge and burn

<Shot name="dodge-burn" alt="A Dodge brush mask with exposure +0.35" menu="Heal, Dodge and Burn are in the dock" />

- **Dodge** and **Burn** start a brush mask that lightens (+0.35 exposure) or darkens (−0.35). Paint, then adjust like any [mask](../masking/).

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **Heal**, **Dodge** and **Burn** are in the dock. Red Eye: **Window ▸ Edit Sections** (or the tool strip in **Side**).
</Aside>
