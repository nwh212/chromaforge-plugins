## Film
- A film scanner's RGBI TIFF (four 16-bit samples, no ExtraSamples, written by VueScan or SilverFast) decodes as opaque RGB with the infrared pass as `Decoded::infrared` (and `SourceInfo::infrared`, through the geometry with `render_plane`), no longer as RGBA.
- Film dust removal from that pass (`FilmDust` in the develop settings: controls `filmDust.remove`, `filmDust.sensitivity`, `filmDust.expand`, Section Sensor): a Source-stage step before the plug-in filters fills the marked pixels in from their surroundings (`lcc_pipeline::film_dust::inpaint`; the map `infrared_map` is cached per pass and settings, and keyed into the stage caches); `Overlay::FilmDust` tints the map. Remove ▸ Remove Film Dust, the two sliders and Show infrared mask.
- `spot.filmDustMask {sensitivity, expand}` (Remove ▸ Show as Mask) stores the pass's marks as an Object mask (a `SegMask` over the uncropped frame, named "Film dust (infrared)", replaced on a re-run, selected) for the brush and `spot.fromMask`. `spot.findFilmDust` (Film Dust Spots) stays for scans without the pass: Fill spots by default (`mode: heal` copies), `expand` grows them, auto-tagged film dust and replaced on a re-run.
