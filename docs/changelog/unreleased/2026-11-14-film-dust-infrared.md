## Film
- A film scanner's RGBI TIFF (four 16-bit samples, no ExtraSamples, written by VueScan or SilverFast) decodes as opaque RGB with the infrared pass as `Decoded::infrared` (and `SourceInfo::infrared`, through the geometry with `render_plane`), no longer as RGBA. `spot.findFilmDust` (Remove ▸ Film Dust) heals the dips of that pass (specks, patches and lines, `lcc_pipeline::film_dust::detect_infrared`), and falls back to the picture-based search (top-hat on the converted positive) without one; `infrared: auto | only | never`. Its spots are tagged auto film dust (`AutoSpotKind::FilmDust`); `replace` (default) makes a run at another `sensitivity` replace them, which the panel's *Film dust sensitivity* slider does live. Spots are Fill (content-aware) by default, `mode: heal` copies from a source, `expand` grows each mark; `spot.filmDustMask {sensitivity, expand}` stores the pass's marks as an Object mask (a `SegMask` over the uncropped frame, named "Film dust (infrared)", replaced on a re-run, selected) for the brush and `spot.fromMask`.
